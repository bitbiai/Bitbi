How we handle your data — GDPR & TDDDG

BITBI Privacy Notice

Version dated 24 October 2026.

1. Controller, scope and contact

The controller for the processing described below is Stefan van Ark, trading as BITBI, Schwarzwaldstraße 20, 78647 Trossingen, Germany. Contact: bit@bitbi.ai. Further provider details are available in the Legal Notice on bitbi.ai.

This notice explains personal-data processing when visiting bitbi.ai and using accounts, AI generation, Canvas projects, saved media, public galleries, social features, organisations, payments and enquiries. Personal data is information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.

For organisation accounts, the organisation's purposes must be distinguished from our own purposes for account management, security, billing and legal duties. An organisation name or team role alone establishes neither a particular data protection role nor a data processing agreement. This notice does not claim a separately agreed processor arrangement for all organisation content.

This notice does not replace separately required consent or a required data processing agreement. Using the website or accepting our terms is not blanket data protection consent.

2. Public browsing and technical delivery

Public pages and galleries are accessible without an account. Technical data includes IP address, time, requested address, browser/device information, transmitted headers and error or security signals. We and our infrastructure provider Cloudflare process the data necessary for delivery, connections, attack protection, abuse prevention and troubleshooting.

The legal basis for secure public delivery is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is operating and protecting a functional website. Article 6(1)(b) GDPR applies where processing is necessary for a contractual feature you request. Analytics purposes are separate from those operational purposes; see section 10.

Cloudflare delivers the website. GitHub is used for source control, tests and release processes and is not the public website host. A normal website visit does not on that account transmit private Canvas prompts to GitHub. Following a GitHub link or contributing to the repository involves separate processing there.

3. Accounts, sign-in and security

We process email address, password hash, verification status, account identifier, roles, sign-in and security information, and session and token data for account registration and use. Passwords are not retained as ordinary plaintext account records. Verification and password-reset links contain single-use tokens; the application stores token hashes server-side.

Processing needed to establish and provide the account relies on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Account protection and abuse investigation, where not already necessary for the contract, rely on Article 6(1)(f). Our legitimate interest is protecting accounts, data and infrastructure.

Session cookies enable sign-in and are Secure and HttpOnly in the intended production environment. The technical session lifetime is 30 days; signing out ends the session or expires the cookie. Verification and password-reset tokens are valid for 60 minutes; wallet challenges for ten minutes. Token validity is not the retention period of the account or related email records. See section 13.

Additional authentication and audit measures protect administrative access. They do not confer unrestricted access to user content: authorised access must serve the relevant lawful operational, support or security purpose.

4. Profiles, wallets, favourites and organisations

You may supply optional profile details, such as a display name, description, avatar and website link. Processing for the chosen profile features relies on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Public visibility depends on the relevant profile and publication feature; a name or avatar may appear with published media and comments.

If you choose wallet linking or wallet sign-in, we process the wallet address, chain/network details, nonces and signature-verification data needed for that feature. Private keys are not transmitted to BITBI for this purpose. Wallet software and public blockchain information are distinct from BITBI's account records; deleting an account does not remove their independent records.

Favourites are processed in the account context. Likes, comments and follow relationships support the corresponding social features; comments and associated profile information may be public. Adding a favourite does not by itself publish a private asset.

Organisation features process names, invitations, member emails, roles, permissions and usage/billing information. Authorised organisation owners can see member email addresses, roles and organisation-related usage and ledger data for team administration and allocation of shared use. This does not make all account content accessible to all members. A contract with the organisation owner is not by itself the basis for every use of an invited person's data. Assigning access rights does not make content generally public. Necessary team administration and access security may rely on our legitimate interest in providing and protecting the requested collaboration under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; your own requested account functions rely on point (b). Affected members retain their rights.

5. AI features, uploads and the Canvas assistant

5.1 Inputs and outputs

For the AI functions you select, we process required prompts, text, reference images and other media, together with settings such as model, style, duration, resolution, aspect ratio and audio options. Outputs may include images, videos, music, text, previews, metadata and technical processing records. Details depend on the model and operation actually used.

Processing of your own personal data necessary to provide the requested service generally relies on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Operations, abuse prevention and necessary legal defence are assessed separately by purpose. Billing and statutory evidence do not automatically require retention of complete raw prompts.

5.2 Project conversations and automated actions

The Canvas assistant processes project chat, the brief, relevant nodes and references, approved media, model choice, budget decisions, execution state and necessary error context. These data are stored with the project. Selected information relevant to the current task is sent to the chosen assistant model. Supported visual checks may include selected video frames.

The assistant may prepare or execute project actions within approved functions. Budget and action records document scope and accounting. An explanation of an error does not automatically authorise another operation.

Private operator/admin chats and summaries remain separate from member projects. They are not imported into your Canvas conversation as general memory. Personal data used in an internal support process still requires an appropriate purpose and legal basis.

5.3 Provider routes

Cloudflare is the infrastructure provider or intermediary in the AI routes described here. For identifiers beginning @cf/…, Cloudflare runs inference. Naming a model developer does not mean BITBI calls that developer's API. External models receive content through Cloudflare AI Gateway; narrowly scoped direct operator routes are listed separately in the provider appendix.

A model does not receive data merely because it is listed. The selected feature, its availability and the actual executed route determine the transfer. The appendix describes input categories and known providers, including the expressly stated limits of recipient identification. Section 11 addresses international transfers.

The actual recipient entity and applicable contractual and transfer chain have not yet been fully confirmed for several external Gateway routes. Appendix A identifies those limits. They establish neither the absence of an agreement nor proof of the required coverage through this notice.

5.4 Other people's and sensitive data

Submit only content you may lawfully use for the relevant purpose. Copyright permissions, personality rights and data protection grounds are distinct. Our contract with you is not a blanket legal basis for all data about other people shown or mentioned in your inputs.

Health data and other special categories, information about children, and face or voice references may require additional safeguards. General generation features and acceptance of terms provide neither a blanket legal basis nor a guarantee of a suitable provider route. Indirect collection is also subject to the applicable information duties, including Article 14 GDPR. A face or voice recording is not by itself biometric processing for unique identification.

5.5 Training and other uses

Inputs are transmitted for the selected function, storage and technical processing, and the separately described protection and record-keeping purposes. A private technical processing licence is not consent to publication, advertising or training our own or third-party models. This notice introduces no additional permission for training or cross-product evaluation.

Cloudflare states for Workers AI that customer content is not used to train models. That service-specific statement does not automatically cover external providers connected through AI Gateway. We do not give a universal no-training or no-improvement promise for all providers. Logging, caching, temporary computation, persistent project storage, security retention and model training are distinct purposes. Disabling Gateway payload logs does not establish that providers retain no data.

5a. Public website assistant

The public website assistant is disabled. It is separate from the active Canvas assistant. No conversation is transmitted for the disabled feature. Any later activation requires information about its actual processing; this notice does not announce activation.

6. Saved media and data management

Saved media includes originals, selected results, derivatives, previews, covers, filenames and technical metadata. Metadata is mainly processed in Cloudflare D1; files are processed in R2 or Cloudflare Images as appropriate. This supports requested storage, display, editing and downloads under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Private assets do not become public merely through creation. Publication uses the designated feature. Authorised operational and support access remains purpose-limited.

Deleting a project, deleting an Assets Manager item and unpublishing are different actions. Independently saved assets may survive deletion of a Canvas project. Removal from the interface does not necessarily mean that every preview, archive copy or necessary billing record has already been physically erased. Section 13 explains retention and deletion.

7. Public galleries, comments and social functions

When you publish media, the selected file, preview, title, type, timestamps and associated profile information may become publicly accessible. Comments and associated profiles may also be public. Third parties may copy or distribute public content outside our control.

We process publication and interactions to provide the feature you request. Necessary moderation and abuse prevention protect people, rights and platform operations; the basis depends on the relevant legal obligation or legitimate protective interest.

Unpublishing removes public presentation in offerings controlled by BITBI; technical caches may have a limited propagation period. It does not recall independent third-party copies. A single confirmed maximum period covering all involved caches is not currently available. This creates no entitlement to indefinite retention or continued publication.

8. Credits, contract records and payments

We process offers, order/payment references, balances, reservations, usage charges, billing periods, contract and consent snapshots, declarations and delivery status. Purposes are contract performance under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, applicable legal obligations under point (c), and proportionate fraud/double-charge prevention under point (f).

Stripe handles payments, subscriptions and, where applicable, payment management. Full card details are processed within the intended Stripe checkout and are not stored as ordinary BITBI records. BITBI receives the payment information needed for matching, fulfilment, accounting and handling enquiries. Stripe may act as processor or independent controller depending on purpose. The Stripe contracting entity applicable to BITBI's account and its complete international processing chain have not yet been confirmed. We identify Stripe as the payment service without assigning an unverified entity or an exclusively processor role; see section 11.

Receipt of withdrawal or termination is recorded separately from later legal assessment, reimbursement or a Stripe change. A technical confirmation-email failure does not erase receipt of a declaration.

9. Contact, transactional emails and declarations

When you contact us, we process your name/contact information, message and necessary technical protection data. Contract-related or pre-contractual enquiries rely on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR; other relevant enquiries rely on our legitimate interest in responding under point (f). Legally required handling and documentation rely on point (c) where the specific duty applies.

Resend, operated by Plus Five Five, Inc., delivers verification, password-reset, contract and declaration confirmations. It receives the necessary destination and message content. We process acceptance, delivery, failure and bounce information for reliable delivery. Provider acceptance or observed delivery is not proof that a person read the message.

Account-specific retention and the delivery service's open/click-tracking settings have not been confirmed. We therefore give neither a specific supplier deletion period nor a promise that every email is free of such measurements. A transactional email is not marketing consent.

10. Cookies, browser storage and analytics

10.1 Purpose-specific storage

Cookies, localStorage and sessionStorage support expressly requested features, including authentication, security proofs, recording your consent choice and reliable recovery of pending user actions. Selected language, wallet, organisation and playback preferences are assessed separately by purpose.

Storage/access strictly necessary to provide a service you expressly request relies on section 25(2) TDDDG. Where no exemption applies, consent under section 25(1) is required before storage/access. The analytics-control limitation described below is separate; this notice does not claim consistently effective consent control. Subsequent personal-data processing also needs the appropriate GDPR basis. A convenience setting is not automatically strictly necessary just because it is technically useful.

The storage appendix lists the BITBI keys used for these features. localStorage does not automatically expire without an application rule. Clearing browser data may affect sign-in, settings or interrupted-operation recovery; it does not erase independently required server data.

10.2 Website performance measurement and a limitation of the choice

Cloudflare delivers a Web Analytics beacon from static.cloudflareinsights.com. Its purpose is measuring page visits and performance, including loading times and Core Web Vitals. The browser can send page/navigation information, timing measurements and technical browser/device information to the RUM endpoint /cdn-cgi/rum; the connection also involves technical connection data. Cloudflare explains its data collection. This performance analysis is distinct from necessary delivery and attack protection.

The current choice in Cookie Settings does not reliably prevent this analytics activity. A check on 24 October 2026 observed beacon loading without a prior choice and despite refused analytics, together with RUM send attempts. Those test requests were intercepted before transmission. They therefore prove neither acceptance nor storage by Cloudflare. Refusal is not presented here as a verified effective analytics opt-out.

The account-specific retention period and complete legal allocation of this analysis have not yet been confirmed. Absence of cookies does not automatically make performance analysis necessary or exempt from consent. This disclosure replaces neither required consent nor correction of the choice control. Your statutory rights, including withdrawal of consent and objection where applicable, remain intact.

We use Cloudflare for infrastructure and technical intermediation, Stripe for payments, Resend for emails, and the AI providers in Appendix A for selected features. GitHub serves source control, testing and release processes. Processing on our behalf, independent control and technical intermediation are different roles. The provider appendix forms part of this section.

Transfers outside the EEA require a permitted transfer mechanism as well as the general legal basis. An applicable adequacy decision may be relevant; otherwise appropriate safeguards such as validly agreed standard contractual clauses under Article 46(2)(c) GDPR and necessary supplementary measures are required, unless a narrowly defined statutory exception applies. Acceptance of our terms does not replace these requirements.

For several external Gateway routes, the exact recipient entity, account-specific contractual chain, processing countries and actually applicable transfer safeguards have not yet been fully confirmed. The European Commission's adequacy decisions for Switzerland and the United Kingdom, with UK adequacy renewed in December 2025, apply within their respective scope and are distinct from onward transfers elsewhere. There is no corresponding general adequacy decision for China, Hong Kong or Singapore. Registered office, storage, support and access countries are different facts. 12. Operations, diagnostics and administrative access

We process purpose-limited activity, security, usage and error information to operate features, troubleshoot, prevent abuse and maintain necessary records. Access is limited to authorised persons/services for the relevant purpose. Depending on that purpose, the basis is Article 6(1)(b), (c) or (f) GDPR; legitimate interests include secure operation, accountable billing and necessary legal defence.

Canvas incidents may be explained by the chosen assistant using limited, sanitised project context. This is not a general transfer of complete infrastructure logs or other users' projects. Operational logs, Gateway logs, project memory and public browser analytics have different purposes and retention rules.

14. Your rights

Subject to legal conditions, you have rights of access (Article 15 GDPR), rectification (16), erasure (17), restriction (18) and portability (20). Portability concerns, in particular, data you provide that is processed automatically on consent or contract grounds; it does not automatically cover every internal record.

Objection: You may object on grounds relating to your particular situation to processing based on Article 6(1)(e) or (f). We will stop that processing unless we demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds overriding your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing concerns establishing, exercising or defending legal claims. You may object to direct-marketing processing at any time without giving reasons; such processing then stops.

You may withdraw consent at any time for the future without affecting prior lawful processing. Exercising rights is generally free. We respond within statutory periods, normally one month; any permitted extension and its reasons will be communicated within that period. Where identity doubts are reasonable, we request only proportionate additional evidence.

You may complain to a supervisory authority, including where you habitually reside, work or believe an infringement occurred. The State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Baden-Württemberg is a competent contact for our establishment.

Data essential for account functions cannot all be omitted while retaining those functions. Optional profile information and optional consent are different. Refusal of optional analytics must not make unrelated contractual functions conditional on consent.

15. Automated processing and decisions

AI features automatically generate content from your inputs. Before execution, technical rules check available credits, reservations, project/model limits, quotas, request rates and permissions, among other criteria. An unmet criterion may prevent an operation or require review. Permanent account-status changes in the available administration route are initiated by a person.

Calling a control “rule-based” does not determine whether Article 22 GDPR applies. We give no blanket assurance that every fraud or security decision falls outside that Article. Where a solely automated decision has legal or similarly significant effects, its requirements and safeguards apply, including human intervention, expressing your position and contesting the decision where provided by law.

Contact bit@bitbi.ai about a restriction or incorrect account state. This contact route does not claim an already completed individual review or an automated appeal or legal-assessment service.

16. Changes to this notice

We update this notice when actual processing or legal requirements change. We provide information about material new purposes before the relevant further processing as legally required. An update does not replace required renewed consent. Publishing a new website version does not rewrite historical contract confirmations.
